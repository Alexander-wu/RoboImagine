Post-training dynamics from a shared 50k-step SFT initialization. (a–d) Metrics are averaged over 32 predicted frames on the same 128 RT-1 trajectories; markers denote evaluated checkpoints at 0, 100, 250, and 500 additional training updates. Curves compare continued SFT, MCTS, ordinary beam, and RoboImagine (GDBS); search variants use combined feedback. (e) A selected drawer-closing trajectory compares continued SFT, MCTS, ordinary beam, and RoboImagine (GDBS) at the three training checkpoints. Every image in (e) shows the fixed prediction frame +32, rather than a different time within a rollout. Ground truth and the initial SFT prediction are shared references. Gold boxes and enlarged details use identical crop coordinates across all checkpoints. The single example illustrates visual differences and is distinct from the aggregate curves.
