Training cost and prediction quality under matched update counts. Continued SFT and RoboAlign-R2 start from the same 50k-step SFT checkpoint and run for 500 additional updates with a global batch size of eight. Training GPU-hours are observed training-loop wall time multiplied by the allocated H20 count (four for SFT and eight for R2); the two runs do not use equal compute. The R2 timer includes candidate search, decoding, visual and VLM feedback, policy updates, synchronization, and checkpoint saving. Both costs exclude preprocessing, initialization, evaluation, and the earlier 50k SFT updates. PSNR changes are relative to the shared SFT initialization, averaged over 32 predicted frames on the same 128 RT-1 trajectories.
